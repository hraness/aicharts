Attribution: **Arena (lmarena-ai), Arena leaderboard dataset**. Original data and revision links accompany each chart. AI Charts selects four overall media cohorts and projects their fields into chart observations. Published ratings, confidence bounds, variances, model labels, publication dates, and vote counts are unchanged. No user-level votes, prompts, or generated media are copied.
